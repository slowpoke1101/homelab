i reset the flint and put it in ap mode. etherenet ports worked but wifi stopped. i was receiving an ip on the 10.1.3.0/25 correctly.
i installed lucy and went to interfaces - devices - add device configuration.
device type:802.1q base device:br-lan vlanID:3 to match opnsense.
i went to interfaces and edit "lan" and changed base device to software-vlan:br-lan.3
it took a few minutes but this enabled connectivity through wifi. im assuming i can bridge an iot network through wifi also this way. will try in the future.
