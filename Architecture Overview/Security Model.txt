Security Model

Inter-VLAN firewalling: Mimi enforces default-deny rules between LAN1, V88, Prod4, Mgmt11, Sandbox50, and IoT99. Only required ports are opened.

Zero‑trust admin access: OPNsense, TrueNAS, Proxmox, Netgear GUI's are restriced to Workstation. No other devices can access the firewall or management interfaces.

IDS rulesets: Basic intrusion detection is enabled to monitor suspicious traffic patterns

Service isolation: netopia is isolated in VLAN4; Joe is isolated in VLAN11; IoT devices are fully contained in VLAN99

Remote Access: Tailscale replaced WireGuard and port forwarding. Only Mimi and LONS are on the tailnet. LONS has access only to Joe SMB and netopia web applications such as Immich, Vaultwarden, and Memos.
