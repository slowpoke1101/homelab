## immich <img src="images/immich.png" alt="png icon" width="24" height="24">

- self-hosted replacement for apple photos
- running on the Docker VM
- exposed through nginx proxy manager
- accessible from iOS and Fedora only, local or remote(tailscale)
![immich route](images/webapp-route.png)
- image library at /mnt/nfs/library
